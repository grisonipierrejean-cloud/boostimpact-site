Who processes your data

BoostImpact is a trading name of Girlden LTD, a company registered in England and Wales under number 14837617, registered office: 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom. Contact for any question about your data: info@boostimpact.agency.

The company is established in the United Kingdom and offers its services to businesses located in France and Belgium. No data protection officer has been appointed to date.

Depending on the data, the company acts in one of two roles:

  • Controller for the data of its business clients, of businesses that request a free audit, and of visitors to this website.
  • Processor, on behalf of the business, for the data of that business's own customers: review requests by email, reviews and comments received, messages left on the business's review page. The controller is then the business; its obligations and ours are set out in the data processing agreement. If you are a customer of a business, please send your requests to that business; if you write to us, we forward your request to it.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its rectification, its erasure, the restriction of its processing and its portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest, in particular to prospecting emails: the unsubscribe link in each email is enough. Write to info@boostimpact.agency. You will receive a reply within the time limits set by the GDPR.

Texts written by artificial intelligence are submitted to the business or governed by its settings; no decision producing legal effects concerning you is made by automated means.

You can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL): cnil.fr/fr/adresser-une-plainte, or, if you are in Belgium, with the Belgian Data Protection Authority: dataprotectionauthority.be. As the company is established in the United Kingdom, the UK authority, the Information Commissioner's Office, can also be contacted: ico.org.uk.

Security

Login to the client area with an email address and a personal password, chosen from a link sent by email. Each business sees only its own data, a rule enforced by the database itself. Access keys to the services never leave the servers. Exchanges are encrypted (https).

Changes

This policy may change; the version in force is dated at the top of this page. Business clients are notified by email of any change that concerns them.
