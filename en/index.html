Your questions.

Are your response times guaranteed?

Yes, three of them. Your Google listing is put in order within 7 days. On Growth and Premium, your first post is proposed within 72 hours. Every new review gets its reply published within 24 hours. If we miss one of these, the following month is free.

The clock starts once you have given us access to your listing. On Growth and Premium, we also need your first photos. If we create your listing, the clock starts once Google has verified it (terms of sale, section 10).

Putting the listing in order covers: categories, description, opening hours (bank holidays included), payment options and services, your menu as text, the booking link, your photos, a first post and replies to reviews from the last 12 months that are still unanswered. We also check that your name, address, phone number and opening hours match on your website, Facebook, Instagram and Apple Maps, and we tell you about any differences.

Your rating depends on your customers. Nobody can honestly guarantee it.

What do I actually have to do?

At the start, you add our address to your Google listing. We guide you step by step. On Growth and Premium, you also add us to your Instagram and Facebook pages, and send us your first photos.

After that, you send photos when you have some. And you approve your posts, from the email or your client area. Review replies go out without you, on all three plans. On Premium, with delegation mode, approving becomes optional.

What is published without me?

On all three plans: the replies to your reviews. They go out without you, in the tone agreed at the start. For a 1- to 3-star review, someone on our team checks the reply, publishes it and lets you know. On Essential and Growth, your posts still wait for your approval.

On Premium, you can also turn on delegation mode in your client area (your agreement is recorded there). A post left without a reply 48 hours after we send it then goes out without you. You can decline it before, and turn the mode off whenever you like, in your client area or with a simple email.

A sensitive review (health, accident, threat, accusation) is always handled by someone on our team: they reply, publish and let you know.

By subscribing, whatever the plan, you authorise us to publish these replies in your name. You can ask to see or correct any of them.

Will an AI reply with any old thing?

No. Our content is based on the information you have approved. No commercial claim (price, offer, allergens...) is published without your consent.

Do I have to give you my passwords?

Never. On your Google listing, you add our address with the “Manager” role. You stay the owner. On Growth and Premium, you also add us to your Instagram and Facebook pages. We guide you step by step, and you can remove us whenever you like.

Pay yearly or monthly?

Yearly, you pay 12 months in advance and each month costs less. On Essential: €79 excl. VAT instead of €95, so €192 less over the year. The yearly subscription renews unless you cancel before the end of the current year. We remind you by email 30 days before. Monthly, you stop whenever you like. You can switch from monthly to yearly at any time, with a simple email. On Growth and Premium, paying yearly also saves you the set-up fee (€190 excl. VAT).

Do you only ask happy customers for reviews?

No. The request goes to all your customers, without filtering, and nobody sets your staff a review quota: Google's rules forbid it.

Do you follow Google's rules?

Yes. We never add keywords to your listing's name: Google can suspend a listing for that. We don't filter reviews, and we promise neither a rating nor the removal of a review.

Will you get me recommended by ChatGPT?

We can't promise that. We make your information complete and identical where these tools can look for it: your Google listing (your menu as text included), Apple Maps and your social accounts. What they answer after that, nobody can guarantee.

Do my photos and content belong to me?

Yes. You remain the owner of your photos, your texts and your brand. The posts, replies and videos prepared for you remain yours to use for your business, even after the contract ends.

What happens if I stop?

We cancel scheduled posts that have not gone out yet, stop sending emails on your behalf and stop using your access. You then remove our access to your Google Business Profile and your social accounts.

What if I need more posts?

Each plan has a number of posts per month. Once you reach it, your new photos stay in your client area and can be used the following month. Anything unused in a month is not carried over.

For more, you move up a plan yourself, from your client area (Plan screen, “Switch to…” button). It applies as soon as you pay. To move down a plan, email us: the change happens at the end of the period already paid.

How do I send my photos and videos?

From your client area, on your phone, up to 10 photos at a time. For Premium videos, a filming guide is waiting for you in the client area.

How much is a better rating worth?

We don't promise it. A study by Michael Luca (Harvard Business School, 2011) on restaurants rated on Yelp gives an order of magnitude: estimate it with our simulator, and read its limits.
