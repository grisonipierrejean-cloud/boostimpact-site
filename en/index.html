Your questions.

Are your response times guaranteed?

Yes. Listing optimised within 7 days. First content proposed within 72 hours (Standard and Premium). Every new review handled within 24 hours: reply published, or submitted for your approval. Otherwise, the following month is free. Times run from when you hand over access and your first photos (terms of sale, section 10). Your rating, on the other hand, depends on your customers: nobody can honestly guarantee it.

What is published without me?

In Essential and Standard, nothing without your approval, except in Standard replies to 4- and 5-star reviews, under the rules approved at set-up. In Premium, you agree in writing at set-up that we may publish without your reply: content you haven't replied to 48 hours after we send it is published. You can decline it before then, and stop this by simple email. In every plan, a sensitive review (health, accident, threat, accusation) is never published automatically.

Will an AI reply with any old thing?

No. Our content is based on the information you have approved. No commercial claim (price, offer, allergens...) is published without your consent.

Do I have to give you my passwords?

Never. You add us as a manager and remove us whenever you like.

Pay yearly or monthly?

Yearly, each month costs you less: €79 excl. VAT instead of €95 excl. VAT for Essential, i.e. €192 saved a year. The subscription renews unless cancelled before the end date. Monthly, you stop whenever you like. You can switch from monthly to yearly at any time.

Do you only ask happy customers for reviews?

No. The request goes to all your customers, without filtering, and nobody sets your staff a review quota: Google's rules forbid it.

How much is a better rating worth?

We don't promise it. A Harvard study gives an order of magnitude for restaurants: estimate it with our simulator, and read its limits.
