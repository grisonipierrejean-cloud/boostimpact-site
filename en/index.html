AI visibility

Some customers no longer search on Google: they ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity where to eat. We correct what these tools read about your restaurant, and we show you what they answer, before and after.

Included with Growth and Premium on yearly billing.

What we do, in detail
  • Your Yelp and TripAdvisor listings claimed with you, and your Apple Maps listing (also used by Siri) completed: the basic listing is already in your plan. You pass on the codes you receive.
  • Your information corrected on PagesJaunes (France), Golden Pages (goudengids.be, Belgium) and the main directories, where we have access. For the rest, a clear list.
  • Your Google profile imported into Bing Places, for Bing and Copilot (Microsoft): our team does it from our agency account, with nothing to do on your side. If you leave, we transfer the listing to you.
  • For your website, if you have one: your menu as text, questions and answers, and a small hidden code that describes your restaurant to Google and AI tools (schema.org), ready for you or your web provider to put on your website.

Our advice, on top: TheFork. In France and Belgium, your customers can also book a TheFork table from ChatGPT. You sign up with TheFork, on their terms; it is not included.

You read the answers yourself, four times.

We suggest 10 questions your customers ask; you approve them. Before any work, we ask them to ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, from your town, three times each. Then we ask exactly the same ones again at 7, 30 and 90 days. You get the real answers, as they are and dated, plus 3 screenshots taken in the apps at the start.

Delivered within 7 days, or refunded. The clock starts once we have all three: your payment, Manager access to your Google profile and your 10 approved questions. Delivered means: everything is corrected or requested and your website texts are handed over; going live on Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor, Apple or Microsoft then depends on them. If AI visibility is included in your subscription (Growth or Premium paid yearly), a delay gives you a free month.

No measured progress at 90 days? If your restaurant is not cited clearly more often than at the start (a gap that chance alone cannot explain: the report does the maths and shows you the figures), we carry on for 90 more days, work and measurements free. Conditions, including keeping our access and no closure longer than 14 days: terms of sale, section 3a.

On Premium with yearly billing, we then ask the same questions again every 90 days while your subscription runs.

Nobody can guarantee what an AI answers: we guarantee the deadline and the measurements, not the answers.

Your questions.

What do I actually have to do?

At the start, you add our email address to your Google profile. We guide you step by step. On Growth and Premium, you also connect the social accounts you chose, and send us your first photos.

After that, you send photos when you have some. And you approve your posts, from the email or your client area; you can also edit the text yourself or ask for a new version of the text, with no limit: new proposal within 48 hours. Review replies go out without you, on all three plans. On Premium, with the “Post without me” option, approving becomes optional.

On Essential, your 4 Google posts of the month are made from your photos: without photos, they are not prepared, and we remind you by email. If you allow it in your account, we can also reuse the photos your business has already published on its Google profile.

What is published without me?

On all three plans: the replies to your reviews. They go out without you, in the tone agreed at the start. For a 1- to 3-star review, our team checks the reply, publishes it and lets you know. On Essential and Growth, your posts still wait for your approval.

On Premium, you can also turn on the “Post without me” option in your client area (your agreement is recorded there). A post left without a reply 48 hours after we send it then goes out without you. You can decline it before, and turn the option off whenever you like, in your client area or with a simple email.

A sensitive review (health, hygiene, accident, threat, accusation) is always handled by our team, and never published without your written OK. For a sensitive review or a 1- to 3-star review, you get the review and the suggested reply on WhatsApp before it is published: reply OK, or WAIT on WhatsApp (or tap “I'll reply by message” in your client area), or write what to change. If we don't hear from you 48 hours after you asked us to wait, we publish the suggested reply (except a sensitive review, always handled by hand).

By subscribing, whatever the plan, you authorise us to publish these replies in your name. You can ask to see or correct any of them.

Will an AI reply with any old thing?

No. Our content is based on the information you have approved. No commercial claim (price, offer, allergens...) is published without your consent. Review replies follow the tone and rules agreed with you at the start, and replies to 1- to 3-star reviews are checked by someone on the team.

Are your response times guaranteed?

Yes, three of them. Your Google profile is put in order within 7 days. On Growth and Premium, your first post is proposed within 72 hours. Every new review gets its reply published within 24 hours of the review reaching us (we check several times a day). If we miss one of these, the following month is free. AI visibility has its own guarantee: delivered within 7 days, otherwise refunded, or a free month if it is included in your subscription.

The clock starts once you have given us access to your profile. On Growth and Premium, we also need your first photos. If we create your profile, the clock starts once Google has verified it (terms of sale, section 10).

Putting the profile in order covers: categories, description, opening hours (bank holidays included), payment options and services, your menu as text, the booking link, your photos, a first post and replies to reviews from the last 12 months that are still unanswered. We also check that your name, address, phone number and opening hours match on your website, Facebook, Instagram and Apple Maps, and we tell you about any differences.

Your rating stays the one your customers give you: we invite all of them to leave a review, and each one gets a reply.

Pay yearly or monthly?

Yearly, you pay 12 months in advance and each month costs less. On Essential: €79 excl. VAT instead of €95, so €192 less over the year. The yearly subscription renews for 12 months unless you cancel before the end of the current period. We remind you by email 30 days before. Monthly, you stop whenever you like. You can switch from monthly to yearly at any time, with a simple email. On Growth and Premium, paying yearly also saves you the set-up fee (€190 excl. VAT) and gives you AI visibility (otherwise €490 excl. VAT, once).

All questions
Do I have to give you my passwords?

Never. On your Google profile, you add our email address with the “Manager” role. You stay the owner. On Growth and Premium, you also connect your social accounts, still with no password. We guide you step by step, and you can remove us whenever you like.

Do you only ask happy customers for reviews?

No. The request goes to all your customers, without filtering, and nobody sets your staff a review quota: Google's rules forbid it.

Do you follow Google's rules?

Yes. We never add keywords to your profile's name: Google can suspend a profile for that. We don't filter reviews, and we promise neither a rating nor the removal of a review.

Will you get me recommended by ChatGPT?

We can't promise that: nobody decides what an AI answers. What we do is work on what ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity read to answer: complete information, and the same everywhere.

In all three plans, that means your Google profile (your menu as text included) and Apple Maps, plus your social accounts on Growth and Premium. AI visibility goes further: Yelp, TripAdvisor, directories, Bing and your website. On Growth and Premium, it is free with yearly billing and paid with monthly billing. On Essential, it is available as an option.

And you judge for yourself: 10 customer questions asked to these three tools before any work, then at 7, 30 and 90 days, with their answers as they are and dated.

Do my photos and content belong to me?

Yes. You remain the owner of your photos, your texts and your brand. The posts, replies and videos prepared for you remain yours to use for your business, even after the contract ends.

What happens if I stop?

We cancel scheduled posts that have not gone out yet, stop sending emails on your behalf and stop using your access. You then remove our access to your Google profile and your social accounts.

What if I need more posts?

Each plan has a number of posts per month. Once you reach it, your new photos stay in your client area and can be used the following month. Anything unused in a month is not carried over.

For more, ask for the plan above from your client area (Plan screen, “Switch to…” button). We confirm it with you by message (WhatsApp or email), then we change your subscription. The new plan counts straight away, including for the current month. You only pay the price difference for the rest of the current period, and your renewal date stays the same. One exception, from Essential paid monthly to Growth or Premium: a €190 excl. VAT set-up fee, once, on the upgrade invoice (waived if you already paid for AI visibility on Essential). To move down a plan, email us: the change happens at the end of the period already paid.

How do I send my photos and videos?

From your client area, on your phone, up to 10 photos at a time. One photo is enough to start, five is ideal. For Premium videos, a filming guide is waiting for you in the client area. We edit the videos from what you send: a few seconds filmed on your phone, or your photos. One photo is enough for a video; a few seconds of footage give a better result. No music is added by default: the video keeps the sound of your clips. Videos appear on Instagram and TikTok: choose at least one of them. If you allow it in your client area, we can also reuse the photos and videos your restaurant has already published. If nothing arrives, we remind you on WhatsApp after 7 days, then after 14 days.
