5. VAT

The Supplier is established in the United Kingdom, outside the European Union. The Service is a supply of services to a taxable person established in France: it is deemed to take place in France (article 259, 1° of the French General Tax Code; article 44 of Directive 2006/112/EC) and French VAT is payable by the Client, as the recipient, under the reverse-charge mechanism (article 283, 2 of the French General Tax Code, deriving from article 196 of Directive 2006/112/EC). Invoices are therefore issued excluding VAT and bear the mention “Autoliquidation” (reverse charge).

The Client provides its SIREN number and, if it has one, its VAT identification number. It declares the VAT due on these invoices under its own scheme; the Supplier does not collect it. A Client that falls under the VAT franchise scheme (franchise en base) checks the treatment that applies to it with its adviser.

Where the Client is a taxable person established in Belgium, the Service is deemed to take place in Belgium (article 21, § 2 of the Belgian VAT Code; article 44 of Directive 2006/112/EC) and Belgian VAT is payable by the Client, as the recipient, under the reverse-charge mechanism (article 51, § 2, first subparagraph, 1° of the Belgian VAT Code; article 196 of Directive 2006/112/EC). Invoices are then issued excluding VAT and bear the mention “Reverse charge”. The Client provides its enterprise number (Crossroads Bank for Enterprises) and its VAT number. It declares the VAT due on these invoices under its own scheme; the Supplier does not collect it. A Client that falls under the small business VAT exemption scheme checks the treatment that applies to it with its adviser.

Supplier's VAT number: Not registered for VAT (supplier not established in the European Union; reverse charge applied by the Client).

  • is the owner or authorised manager of the Google listing and of the social media accounts it entrusts;
  • guarantees that the information it gives when ordering and in its client area is accurate and up to date (opening hours, services, prices, claims such as “home-made” or “organic”): content is based on the information it has approved, and no commercial claim (price, offer, allergens) is published without its consent;
  • holds the rights to the photos and videos it uploads, including the consent of identifiable people who appear in them (face or voice), staff and customers alike, to appear in content published on its business's accounts;
  • only imports, for a review request, genuine customers of its business who have agreed to receive that email, and can prove it; it confirms this consent at each import;
  • displays, if it wishes, the review cards provided by the Supplier;
  • remains responsible for the activity of its business and for what it approves.

Publishing rule. Essential: nothing is published without the Client's approval. Growth and Premium: replies to reviews are published for the Client, with nothing for the Client to do: replies to 4- and 5-star reviews within 24 hours, under the rules approved by the Client at set-up; replies to 1- to 3-star reviews by the Supplier's team, which checks the reply written in the tone approved at set-up, publishes it within 24 hours and informs the Client (section 9). In Growth, content is published only after the Client's approval. The Client approves, edits or declines what is submitted to it; without a reply, nothing is published and it receives reminders. Premium: the Client chooses and signs delegation mode at set-up; its approval becomes optional for routine content: content submitted and left without a reply 48 hours after it was sent is deemed approved, then scheduled and published. The Client may decline it before then, and leave delegation mode by email. In all plans, a sensitive review is never published automatically (section 9).

Mandate to publish replies to reviews. By subscribing to the Growth or Premium plan, the Client authorises the Supplier to publish replies to its business's Google reviews in its name, in the tone approved at set-up, under the rules of this section and of section 9. The Client may at any time ask to see a published reply or to have it corrected.

9. Reviews: rules applied

  • No fake reviews. The Supplier never writes a review, neither for the Client nor against a competitor, and refuses any request to do so.
  • No filtering. Reviews are requested from all customers, with no selection based on their satisfaction; the review page is the same whatever the rating and the link to Google is always shown, including when the customer chooses to write privately. No benefit is granted in exchange for a review. Neither the Supplier nor the Client sets the business's staff a number of reviews to obtain, and no customer is pressed to leave a review on the premises. These rules follow Google's rules for contributions to Google Maps.
  • Sensitive review. A review whose content concerns health, an accident, a serious accusation or a threat, whatever its rating, is never handled or published automatically: a person at the Supplier takes it on and the Client is informed, by WhatsApp if it has chosen this channel in its account, otherwise by email; the Client does not need to reply to it. In Essential, that person proposes the reply to the Client and only publishes it with its consent. In Growth and Premium, that person replies and publishes the reply within 24 hours, without the Client's prior approval; the Client is informed and receives the published text.
  • Replies to reviews. Essential: each reply is prepared in the Client's tone and proposed to it; the Client approves, edits or declines it, with reminders 4 hours and then 24 hours after the proposal; it is published after the Client's approval. Growth and Premium: replies to 4- and 5-star reviews are published without prior approval, under the rules approved at set-up; 1- to 3-star reviews are handled by the Supplier's team as soon as they arrive: the reply is written in the Client's tone, checked by a person on the team and published, without the Client's prior approval; the Client is informed and receives the published text (by WhatsApp if it has chosen this channel, otherwise by email).
  • Each review request email states that it is sent on the Client's behalf and carries an unsubscribe link, honoured for all subsequent requests.

10. Guarantee on response times

The Supplier guarantees three response times:

  • the Client's Google Business Profile is optimised within 7 days;
  • in Growth and Premium, the first piece of content is proposed to the Client within 72 hours;
  • every new Google review is handled within 24 hours of arriving in the Service, which checks for new reviews several times a day: the reply is published, or submitted for the Client's approval.

Starting point of these times:

  • on Essential, the date on which the Client has given the Supplier manager access to its Google listing; photos are optional on this plan and do not delay the start;
  • on Growth and Premium, the date on which the Client has given the Supplier manager access to its Google listing and its first photos, whichever is later.

Listing created by the Supplier. If the business does not have a Google Business Profile yet, the Supplier creates it with the Client; this is included in all plans. Google then verifies the listing using the method it chooses (for example a video of the business, a call or a code sent by post), with the Client's participation. In that case, the guaranteed times only start on the date on which Google has verified the listing, never before: that date replaces the date of the manager access in the starting points above. The time Google takes to verify the listing is not guaranteed.

What listing optimisation covers. Using manager access, based on the information and photos provided by the Client, and insofar as Google offers it for the business's category, the Supplier:

  • checks the address, phone number and website;
  • checks that the name, address, phone number and opening hours are the same on the listing and, where they exist, on the website, the Facebook page, the Instagram account and Apple Maps, and reports any differences to the Client; it only changes what the Client has given it access to;
  • chooses the most accurate primary category and, where relevant, a few secondary categories that match a real activity;
  • writes the business description;
  • fills in the opening hours, including special hours already known (bank holidays, closures);
  • fills in the attributes (payment methods, accessibility, terrace, parking...);
  • enters the menu or the services as text, not only as a photo, with their prices when the Client has provided them;
  • adds the booking or ordering links the Client already uses;
  • adds the photos provided by the Client;
  • prepares a first post;
  • prepares replies to reviews from the last 12 months that have not been answered (older reviews are handled afterwards, with no guaranteed time).

The listing's name remains the business's real name: the Supplier adds no keyword, city or slogan to it, in line with Google's guidelines. The post and the review replies are published under the publishing rule in section 8; when they await the Client's approval, the 7-day time applies to their proposal. The time Google takes to review a change does not count towards this time.

Compensation. If one of these times is missed, the month following the missed deadline is free for the Client: that month's subscription is not invoiced or, with annual billing, the paid period is extended by one month. Several deadlines missed in the same month give right to a single free month. This guarantee gives rise to no cash refund.

Exclusions. The guarantee does not apply during a suspension of the Service (section 6), a pause requested by the Client, an outage or a decision of Google or a social network that prevents action, or when the delay is caused by the Client (access withdrawn or not given, information requested and not provided). The Supplier guarantees neither the Client's rating, nor its number of reviews, audience or revenue.
