9. Reviews: rules applied

  • No fake reviews. The Supplier never writes a review, neither for the Client nor against a competitor, and refuses any request to do so.
  • No filtering. Reviews are requested from all customers, with no selection based on their satisfaction; the review page is the same whatever the rating and the link to Google is always shown, including when the customer chooses to write privately. No benefit is granted in exchange for a review. Neither the Supplier nor the Client sets the business's staff a number of reviews to obtain, and no customer is pressed to leave a review on the premises. These rules follow Google's rules for contributions to Google Maps.
  • Sensitive review. A review whose content concerns health, hygiene, an accident, a serious accusation or a threat, whatever its rating, is never handled or published automatically: a person at the Supplier takes it on and sends the Client, by WhatsApp, the review and the suggested reply; the Client does not need to reply to it on Google. In all plans, the reply is published only with the Client's written OK, within 24 hours of that OK. If there is no answer from the Client 48 hours after the alert, the team follows up and decides with the Client; nothing is published automatically.
  • Replies to reviews. In all plans: replies to 4- and 5-star reviews are published without prior approval, under the rules approved at set-up; 1- to 3-star reviews and sensitive reviews are handled by the Supplier's team as soon as they arrive: the reply is written in the Client's tone and checked by a person on the team. Alert before publishing. The Supplier undertakes to send the Client, by WhatsApp, BEFORE any publication, the review and the suggested reply, with the time from which it will be published. The Client's WhatsApp number is required for the Service; each alert is logged, and a failed send is handled by the team. With no answer from the Client, the reply to a 1- to 3-star review is published at the stated time, within the time limit of section 10.
  • I'll reply by message. For a sensitive review or a 1- to 3-star review, as long as the reply has not been published, the Client may ask us to wait, by replying to the alert by message (OK, WAIT, or a correction) or by tapping “I'll reply by message” in its client area. No reply is then published until the team has read the message, and the 24-hour time limit of section 10 is suspended until then, after which it runs again for 24 hours. If we don't hear from you 48 hours after your request, we publish the suggested reply (except a sensitive review, always handled by hand). A sensitive review is never published without the Client's written OK; its 24-hour time limit runs from that OK. For a review, the Supplier does not undertake to make any phone call.
  • Fair use. Replies to reviews are included for up to 100 reviews received per calendar month and per business, in Central European Time. Beyond that, replies continue; the Supplier may offer the Client a plan suited to that volume. Nothing extra is charged without the Client's written agreement.
  • Each review request email states that it is sent on the Client's behalf and carries an unsubscribe link, honoured for all subsequent requests.
