Original content. The cap is counted in pieces of original content per month, never per week. A business subscribed to a plan that is no longer offered keeps its plan and its caps, on the same terms, for as long as its subscription runs.

5. VAT

The Supplier is established in the United Kingdom, outside the European Union. The Service is a supply of services to a taxable person established in France: it is deemed to take place in France (article 259, 1° of the French General Tax Code; article 44 of Directive 2006/112/EC) and French VAT is payable by the Client, as the recipient, under the reverse-charge mechanism (article 283, 2 of the French General Tax Code, deriving from article 196 of Directive 2006/112/EC). Invoices are therefore issued excluding VAT and bear the mention “Autoliquidation” (reverse charge).

The Client provides its SIREN number and, if it has one, its VAT identification number. It declares the VAT due on these invoices under its own scheme; the Supplier does not collect it. A Client that falls under the VAT franchise scheme (franchise en base) checks the treatment that applies to it with its adviser.

Where the Client is a taxable person established in Belgium, the Service is deemed to take place in Belgium (article 21, § 2 of the Belgian VAT Code; article 44 of Directive 2006/112/EC) and Belgian VAT is payable by the Client, as the recipient, under the reverse-charge mechanism (article 51, § 2, first subparagraph, 1° of the Belgian VAT Code; article 196 of Directive 2006/112/EC). Invoices are then issued excluding VAT and bear the mention “Reverse charge”. The Client provides its enterprise number (Crossroads Bank for Enterprises) and its VAT number. It declares the VAT due on these invoices under its own scheme; the Supplier does not collect it. A Client that falls under the small business VAT exemption scheme checks the treatment that applies to it with its adviser.

Supplier's VAT number: Not registered for VAT (supplier not established in the European Union; reverse charge applied by the Client).

  • holds the rights to the photos and videos it uploads, including the consent of identifiable people who appear in them (face or voice), staff and customers alike, to appear in content published on its business's accounts;
  • only imports, for a review request, genuine customers of its business who have agreed to receive that email, and can prove it; it confirms this consent at each import;
  • displays, if it wishes, the review cards provided by the Supplier;
  • remains responsible for the activity of its business and for what it approves.

Publishing rule. Essential and Standard: nothing is published without the Client's approval, except, in Standard, replies to 4- and 5-star reviews, published automatically under the rules approved by the Client at set-up. The Client approves, edits or declines what is submitted to it; without a reply, nothing is published and it receives reminders. Premium: the Client chooses and signs delegation mode at set-up; its approval becomes optional for routine content: content submitted and left without a reply 48 hours after it was sent is deemed approved, then scheduled and published. The Client may decline it before then, and leave delegation mode by email. In all plans, a sensitive review is never published automatically (section 9).

9. Reviews: rules applied

  • No fake reviews. The Supplier never writes a review, neither for the Client nor against a competitor, and refuses any request to do so.
  • No filtering. Reviews are requested from all customers, with no selection based on their satisfaction; the review page is the same whatever the rating and the link to Google is always shown, including when the customer chooses to write privately. No benefit is granted in exchange for a review. Neither the Supplier nor the Client sets the business's staff a number of reviews to obtain, and no customer is pressed to leave a review on the premises. These rules follow Google's rules for contributions to Google Maps.
  • Sensitive review. A review whose content concerns health, an accident, a serious accusation or a threat, whatever its rating, is never handled or published automatically: a person at the Supplier takes it on. In Essential and Standard, that person proposes the reply to the Client and only publishes it with its consent; in Premium, that person replies and consults the Client when needed.
  • Replies to reviews. Essential: each reply is prepared and proposed to the Client, published after its approval. Standard: replies to 4- and 5-star reviews are published without prior approval, under the rules approved at set-up; replies to 1- to 3-star reviews are proposed to the Client, who approves, edits or declines them, with reminders 4 hours and then 24 hours after the proposal. Premium: replies to 4- and 5-star reviews are published in the same way; 1- to 3-star reviews are handled by the Supplier's team as soon as they arrive.
  • Each review request email states that it is sent on the Client's behalf and carries an unsubscribe link, honoured for all subsequent requests.

10. Guarantee on response times

The Supplier guarantees three response times:

Times run from the date on which the Client has given the Supplier manager access to its Google listing and its first photos, whichever is later.

Compensation. If one of these times is missed, the month following the missed deadline is free for the Client: that month's subscription is not invoiced or, with annual billing, the paid period is extended by one month. Several deadlines missed in the same month give right to a single free month. This guarantee gives rise to no cash refund.

Exclusions. The guarantee does not apply during a suspension of the Service (section 6), a pause requested by the Client, an outage or a decision of Google or a social network that prevents action, or when the delay is caused by the Client (access withdrawn or not given, information requested and not provided). The Supplier guarantees neither the Client's rating, nor its number of reviews, audience or revenue.
